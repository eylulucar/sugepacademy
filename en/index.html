PROGRAMMES

Programmes and areas of work

Three ways for young people to explore local needs and develop their own solutions.

Young Sustainability Leaders Programme

Students tackle everyday challenges in İzmir through projects linked to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

Programme details

Students choose a question to investigate in the field, record their observations and present possible solutions. Their research reports and presentations become part of their academic portfolios.

Field journal

Sustainability Ambassadors Programme

Young ambassadors share what they learn with their peers to bring sustainability into school life.

Programme details

Students discuss a need they have noticed at school, agree on a shared goal and design campaigns and workshops together.

Field journal

Youth Action Hub

At the Youth Action Hub, teams from different disciplines take their field observations into the workshop.

Programme details

Teams test ideas on a small scale. They help each other by sharing prototypes, material choices and field notes.

ABOUT US

From knowledge to actionRethinking how we learn

Umut Dilsiz founded SÜGEP Academy in İzmir to help young people contribute to the Global Goals in their own communities.

Findings from fieldwork and workshops can become draft academic papers and reports for international publications. Students applying to universities abroad can use this work to document their own contributions in an academic portfolio.

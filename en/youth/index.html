01 / Founding statement

Developing solutions today.

Climate change, biodiversity loss, shrinking resources, pollution and social inequality are closely connected.

Addressing them calls for sharing knowledge and developing new ways of thinking, doing research, designing technology and putting ideas into practice.

SÜGEP Youth brings scientific thinking, collaboration across disciplines, entrepreneurship and sustainability together so young people can develop practical responses to real-world problems.

“We help young people turn ideas into projects, measure the difference their projects make and build on one another’s work.”

Our purpose · SÜGEP Youth
02 / Our shared values

Eight principles for working together.

Our work is guided by scientific integrity, youth leadership and the common good.

01

Youth leadership

Young people lead decisions, represent the network and develop projects.

02

Scientific integrity

We value accuracy, evidence and ethical research.

03

Eco-innovation

We develop new, practical responses to environmental and social challenges.

04

Collaboration

We share skills and work towards results we can achieve together.

05

Inclusion

We support equal opportunities and welcome young people from different disciplines.

06

Independence

We protect independent thinking, free expression and scientific inquiry.

07

Transparency

We manage decisions, activities and financial resources openly.

08

Global responsibility

We look at local issues from a global perspective and take shared responsibility.

04 / Network development · 2027–2030

How the network will work together.

SÜGEP Youth is designed as a place to develop ideas, carry out research, form teams, build prototypes and share solutions with partners.

01 / DISCOVER

Explore and investigate

Identify a real need, ask a scientific question and draw on more than one discipline to investigate it.

02 / CREATE

Design and develop

Form a team, test ideas and develop prototypes and models that can be put to use.

03 / TRANSFORM

Share what you learn

Show how your solution works, collaborate with partners and share results and lessons learned.

Plans for 2027–2030

2027

Establish the network

Define founding principles, involve young people and develop a structure for collaboration.

2028

Projects and partnerships

Build interdisciplinary teams, introduce mentoring and run pilot projects.

2029

Growth and visibility

Refine solutions, share them more widely and build international exchanges.

2030

Impact and evaluation

Review what teams have learned and improve how they measure their impact.

05 / Independence and public benefit

Working together for the common good.

SÜGEP Youth aims to operate as a non-profit youth network focused on public benefit, social impact and active participation by young people.

Scientific ethics, inclusion, accountability, independent thought and transparency guide the network’s work. Its formal structure and legal status are to be defined separately in accordance with applicable law and its founding documents.

“Young people can take the questions they explore in class and help solve problems in the world around them.”
